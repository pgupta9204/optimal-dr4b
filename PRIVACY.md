### Commitment to Student Privacy & Safety
This application is an educational, open-source engineering tool developed for the VEX Robotics community (including middle school, high school, and university students, educators, and mentors). We believe privacy is a fundamental right, especially for young roboticists and students.

Our telemetry architecture is built with a **Privacy-by-Design** philosophy:
- **100% Zero-Cookie**: We do not set, store, or access tracking cookies on your device (`document.cookie` is never used). No cookie banners or popups are needed because no tracking cookies exist.
- **COPPA Compliant (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)**: We collect **zero personally identifiable information (PII)**. We never ask for or store your name, email address, physical location, school affiliation, or age.
- **GDPR & UK GDPR Compliant**: We never log, store, or sell raw IP addresses. Any network-level edge routing drops IP addresses immediately.
- **Client-Side Physics Isolation**: All kinematics calculations, simulated annealing optimizations, and Engineering Notebook report generations run **100% locally in your web browser**. Your robot CAD parameters, custom arm dimensions, and team mechanism designs never leave your computer.

---

### What Anonymous Information We Measure
To evaluate the mathematical solver's performance, understand community adoption, and report open-source impact, we collect aggregate, non-personally identifiable telemetry:

1. **Anonymous Interaction Events**:
   - Linkage preset selected (e.g., `dr64`, `dr4b`, `single6b`).
   - Optimizer solver completions (e.g., solve duration, percentage torque reduction achieved).
   - Engineering Notebook markdown exports and Pit Build Sheet copies.
   - Config URL share clicks.
   - Unit toggle selections (Inches vs VEX Holes).
2. **Aggregated Site Traffic**:
   - High-level referral source (e.g., whether you discovered the tool via VEX Forum, Chief Delphi, Reddit, or Discord).
   - Coarse geographic region (country or state-level, with zero precise geolocation).
   - Display screen resolution for responsive layout optimization.

### Automated Opt-Out (Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control)
We respect your browser's privacy signals. If your browser sends:
- **Do Not Track (`DNT: 1`)**, or
- **Global Privacy Control (`GPC: true`)**

All telemetry and analytics are **completely suppressed and disabled automatically**.
